# HW Notes in CSCI 1100 

## Context
Do not take notes in class if you can help it! Notes are for homework; you watch lecture videos, listen to lecture audios, and complete technical homeworks **before class**, and you take these notes as you watch, listen, and work.
## Allowed Resources

Use **any resources** you like to attempt to answer your own questions- human, AI, internet, etc.
## Structure
Structure however makes sense best to you. See the [Example HW Notes PDF](../assets/pdfs/HWN1-Gardella.pdf) for one way you could do it. Feel free to adapt your structure over the term.

## Length & Neatness

Do not write a book or a publication-ready diagram! Most submissions should be 1-3 pages only, but there is no strict upper limit. Practice being as concise as possible. Be legible but write and draw whatever makes sense to you (as long as you can decode it when you look back at it in the future).

See also some [example notes](../assets/pdfs/15-Minutes-of-Notes.pdf) I took during a 15 minute conference presentation.
## Content
You should be noting:
- questions that came into your mind **as** you watched, listened, and worked
	- some you answered yourself
	- others you need to ask in class
- points of confusion or surprise

## PDF Preparation

Please use a standard US letter sized PDF pages, with writing going left-to-right (not turned sideways). See acceptable [example 1](../assets/pdfs/HWN1-Gardella.pdf) and [example 2](../assets/pdfs/15-Minutes-of-Notes.pdf).

You can do something like paste photos into Microsoft Word ([example 1](../assets/pdfs/HWN1-Gardella.pdf)) or with Mac/iPhone use Preview app → File menu → import from iPhone -> scan documents ([example 2](../assets/pdfs/15-Minutes-of-Notes.pdf)). Then export as as a **single** PDF with 1 or more pages.

Keep margins small and text legible. While multi-page PDF are allowed, try to be as concise as possible.

See this [Example HW Notes PDF](../assets/pdfs/HWN1-Gardella.pdf) for expected PDF quality and a possible way to structure your notes.

## Submission & Grading

Submit in [Canvas](https://wlu.instructure.com/courses/18744) to the appropriate graded survey to earn an *in-trust* participation grade (see [Syllabus](./Syllabus.md)) for legitimate good faith notes that follow these guidelines.
## Honesty

If you forget to do this or don't plan to follow instructions; take the 0% rather than fabricating fake notes after the fact or submitting garbage. Never double submit the same notes.
